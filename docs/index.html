What am I looking at?

The explorer follows standardized daytime and nighttime CO₂-trap intervals and their catches, including supported zero catches; missed or unusable intervals remain unavailable, never zero. It shows whole-trap-scaled encounters per 24 trap-hours—an activity index, not population size, infection, transmission, exposure, or disease risk. Weather patterns are descriptive, not causal.
