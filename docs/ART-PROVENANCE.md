## Final prompt

> Create a single wide editorial screenprint-style hero illustration for a scientific web app called NEON Mosquito Pulse. Landscape 3:2 composition, designed so the left 42% can be dark and relatively quiet for overlaid HTML headline while the visual focus lives on the center-right. Scene: a moonlit North American wetland / prairie pothole at blue hour after summer rain, layered reeds, pooled water, distant storm clouds, a slim crescent moon and subtle rain texture. Show a scientifically plausible CDC-style CO2 mosquito trap on a shepherd's hook: insulated bait cooler or CO2 canister above, small visible CO2 hose/vent, fan housing and mesh catch cup below, but absolutely NO light bulb, NO glowing lamp, NO UV light. Include one elegant, anatomically recognizable mosquito in the foreground as a graphic field-guide silhouette, not cute, not monstrous. Art direction: refined hand-pulled screenprint and linocut, limited ink palette of deep midnight indigo, storm violet, misty cyan, acid chartreuse accents, and warm paper cream; bold irregular ink shapes, halftone grain, beautiful negative space, contemporary natural-history editorial poster, cinematic but static. Keep the trap recognizable without making it dominate. No people, no blood, no pathogen icons, no medical imagery, no warning symbols, no maps, no charts, no logos, no watermark, and absolutely no words, letters, numerals, labels, or typographic marks anywhere. High contrast and sophisticated, cohesive with an art-forward science explorer suite.

The artwork is illustrative and is labeled as such in both cover surfaces. Scientific claims and trap details come from the cited NEON protocol and product documentation, not from the generated image.
